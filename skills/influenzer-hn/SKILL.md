# influenzer-hn

I play Hacker News. Costume: seminar. Game: curiosity auction plus gravity. Tryable thing, not a launch post.

Median Show HN is about 2 points. I need organic first-hour votes and a curious thread. I never solicit upvotes, stars, follows, or RTs (ban / domain penalty).

Show HN is three fields or silence: `Show HN: …`, URL in the URL field, first comment as backstory. Not a blog, not a waitlist, not a deck, not a Linktree, not a Drive/Dropbox/WeTransfer file, not "please upvote". Human backstory, not a press release. Title + URL without a first comment is not Show HN. A lab notebook is not Show HN: `story_kind` exploration / decision / failure does not sit here, even with a demo. Workshop or silence. Seminar only when a stranger can click and run a major or hard-issue ship. A pitch, PDF of slides, or Notion one-pager without a clickable product is silence. A Linktree, Carrd, bio site, or list of links instead of a product is silence. A Google Drive, Dropbox, or WeTransfer file is a file, not a product.

## Wave

- Title starts with Show HN and a working demo. One line, not a blog. No waitlist, no blog-as-Show, no deck-as-Show, no linktree-as-Show, no Drive-as-Show, no Dropbox-as-Show, no WeTransfer-as-Show. Press-release tone (we’re excited, announcement, unveiling, delighted to share) is kill, not Show HN. `story_kind` exploration / decision / failure does not sit, even with a demo: workshop or silence. Not live.
- URL in the URL field (text posts eat nourl-factor; "Show HN is 0.4x" is usually a misread).
- First comment = backstory. One comment, not a dump of leftover facts. I camp the thread. Human username.
- Three fields or silence. A blog, a waitlist, a deck, a Linktree, a Drive/Dropbox/WeTransfer file, or "please upvote" is silence.
- Timing folklore contradicts itself. Not a cheat code.
- Ignore playbooks that say pre-seed 5-10 friends.
