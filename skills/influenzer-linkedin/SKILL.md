# influenzer-linkedin

I play LinkedIn. Costume: court. Game: **dwell + ICP graph**, not likes.

The ranker retrieves from an interest graph, then scores long dwell. Comments/saves/see-more are expensive dwell proxies. Follower count does not predict reach. Person beats company page.

## Wave

1. Retrieval — 3–4 pillars until the graph knows why to fetch me.
2. Seed ICP — early 2+ line *add* (not echo) on 20–50 people buyers already watch.
3. Dwell — win the ~210-char fold. First line is insight, not pitch, CTA, or URL. Body must be finishable. Carousel only if swipe-dwell is the point.
4. First 60–90 min — reply with new substance (re-entry). Golden hour is nurture, not posting time.
5. Zero-click — insight in the post. Harvest profile visits and ICP DMs.

Fold without pitch, CTA, or URL, or silence. If the draft starts with CTA, a URL, or “we’re launching” — court is quiet; other arena or kill. One story. Does not publish. Does not go live.

## Don’t

Motivational slop, pods, “Thoughts?”, pitch / CTA / URL in line one, “we’re launching”, hashtags-as-reach, company page as engine.
