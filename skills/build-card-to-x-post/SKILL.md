# build-card-to-x-post

Turn a build card into one short draft post for X.

Rules:

- Draft only; do not publish.
- Mention the project and concrete maintainer decision.
- Keep the voice useful, technical, and build-in-public.
- Avoid hype and direct library promotion.
- Include one tradeoff or question when possible.
- Do not include secrets, private paths, or credentials.
