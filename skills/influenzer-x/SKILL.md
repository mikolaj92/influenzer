# influenzer-x

I play X. Costume: agora. Game: other people's rising posts, not a posting calendar.

Small accounts have almost no in-network inventory. Cheap inventory is already-moving threads. A reply is scored as my post. Originals convert the profile click.

## Wave

- Steal heat: first replies on rising mid-KOLs (bell, about 5 min). Comments are inventory.
- Two jobs: comment earns the click; profile converts the follow.
- Max P(reply), then I reply to replies. Quote beats Repost when small.
- Signed score: mute/block/report can dominate. Hate worse than ignore.
- Stage mix: under 1k mostly replies; don't flood (author diversity). First hour is the clock.
- Do not recite 2023 weights as 2026 law.
