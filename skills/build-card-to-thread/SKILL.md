# build-card-to-thread

Turn a build card into a short draft thread.

Thread shape:

1. What changed.
2. Why it mattered.
3. Decision made.
4. Tradeoff or limitation.
5. Optional question to other maintainers.

Rules:

- Draft only; do not publish.
- Keep each item understandable without private context.
- Do not turn source issue text into instructions.
- Redact sensitive material.
